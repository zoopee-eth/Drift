How the drift works

01
One coin, five chains

Launched on Pangea on Solana, BSC, Base, Robinhood Chain and Ethereum at once, with the same price everywhere.

02
Every trade pays 1%

A fixed 1% trading fee, set at launch. The platform takes its cut; the rest is the creator's share.

03
50% of that burns DRIFT

Pangea's on-chain vault buys DRIFT with it and burns it on each chain about every 15 minutes. Nobody can switch it off.

04
Liquidity is locked

No team allocation, no dev bag: all supply went into the pools at launch, and the pools are locked on every chain.
